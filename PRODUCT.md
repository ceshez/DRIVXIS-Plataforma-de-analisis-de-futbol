# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Coaches, video analysts, and academy staff use DRIVXIS while reviewing football matches. Their primary task is to upload footage, track the analysis status, and quickly review tactical and physical metrics for training and reporting.

## Product Purpose

DRIVXIS turns match video into a durable analysis workflow: upload a recording, process it with computer vision, and inspect the resulting video and metrics in one place. Success means that analysis state, progress, failures, and results are clear without interrupting the user’s work.

## Brand Personality

Direct, technical, and competitive. The interface should communicate focus and high-performance analysis without becoming playful or decorative.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic startup styling, blue or purple gradients, overly rounded SaaS cards, excessive icon decoration, and large visual treatments that reduce the density or scanability of football analysis data.

## Design Principles

1. Make analysis state and the next user action unambiguous.
2. Prioritize dense, scannable information for match-review workflows.
3. Keep the interface technically focused so content and evidence lead the screen.
4. Preserve consistent interaction patterns across upload, analysis, history, and account pages.
5. Prefer feedback that preserves layout and context while work is in progress.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast on dark surfaces, visible keyboard focus, descriptive loading feedback for assistive technology, and reduced-motion support. Do not convey analysis status through color alone.
